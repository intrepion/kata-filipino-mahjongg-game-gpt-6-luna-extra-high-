A TABLE GUIDE

Filipino Mahjong,
the way we play it.

Filipino Mahjong has family and regional variations. This table uses one documented Philippine 16-tile profile; Jokers and pot rules are off.

01

Make a hand

Keep 16 suited tiles between turns. Win with five sets and a pair. A set is a Chow (three in a row, same suit) or Pung (three alike). A Kang is four alike and needs a replacement draw. Mano passes clockwise each hand.

02

Mind your Flores

Winds, Dragons, Flowers, and Seasons are Flores here. Reveal one when drawn, then replace it from the back of the wall. Only Balls, Sticks, and Characters make sets.

03

Call with care

Chow only from the player before you. Pung and Kang may use any discard. Todas (win on a discard) outranks Kang, then Pung, then Chow.

04

Win & score

Bunot is a self-drawn win; Todas is a discard win. Both 5 sets + a pair and Siete Pares (7 pairs + a set) are accepted. Todas charges the discarder 2× and others 1×; Bunot charges all 2×. Secret Kang pays 1 point per opponent. Flush, Puro Pung, concealed, no Flores, Siete Pares, and Escalera add multipliers.
